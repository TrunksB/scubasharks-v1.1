OUR CAPABILITIES

Services below the surface.

We are fully equipped to provide a comprehensive range of diving services to undertake any kind of underwater work. Our teams are led by highly talented and industry experienced people and supported by very competent personnel, trained to meet international standards and certified by both CONFEDERATION MONDIALE DES ACTIVITES SUBAQUATIQUES (CMAS) and Freediving Coaches of Asia (FCOA).

01

TRAINING

Swimming Training

Progressive swimming instruction for learners who want to develop water confidence, technique, endurance and safety awareness. Certificates are available for successful completion of applicable training programmes.

  • Water familiarisation and confidence
  • Stroke and technique development
  • Endurance and aquatic fitness
  • Safety-oriented swimming skills
02

TRAINING

Diving Training

Learn essential diving knowledge and practical skills through structured instruction. Training can be tailored to the learner's experience and objectives, with certification awarded where the relevant course requirements are successfully completed.

  • Beginner and skills-development instruction
  • Water confidence and dive safety fundamentals
  • Practical sessions and supervised skills
  • Course completion and certificates as applicable
03

COMMERCIAL DIVING

Underwater Inspections

Underwater visual inspections help clients understand the condition of submerged structures and equipment without relying solely on dry access.

  • Submerged structures and facilities
  • Marine and freshwater equipment
  • Visual condition assessment
  • Photo/video documentation where practical
04

UNDERWATER WORKS

Underwater Installations

Support for planned underwater installation tasks, subject to site assessment, equipment requirements and safe operating conditions.

  • Positioning and securing underwater equipment
  • Installation support for submerged systems
  • Site preparation and task planning
  • Post-installation checks where applicable
05

MAINTENANCE

Underwater Cleaning & Maintenance

Practical underwater cleaning and maintenance support for submerged assets and facilities. The exact method is selected according to the material, condition and site environment.

  • Removal of aquatic growth and deposits
  • Cleaning of submerged surfaces
  • Routine underwater maintenance support
  • Condition observations during service
06

RECOVERY

Shallow Water Search & Recovery

Organised shallow-water search and recovery operations for suitable assignments, with attention to search planning, diver safety and communication.

  • Search planning and area assessment
  • Systematic shallow-water searches
  • Recovery of suitable objects
  • Operational reporting where required
07

SAFETY TRAINING

Emergency Response Training

Practical training designed to help teams prepare for aquatic incidents and respond more effectively while awaiting or coordinating appropriate emergency assistance.

  • Emergency preparedness principles
  • Response roles and communication
  • Aquatic incident scenarios
  • Team drills and practical exercises
