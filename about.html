ABOUT SCUBA SHARKS UGANDA

Passion for water.
Professionalism in every dive.

Based in Buikwe, Uganda, we provide diving, aquatic training and underwater service solutions with a practical, safety-focused approach. we specialize in providing underwater services to our offshore (Oil & Gas), onshore, Civil, Marine and Shipping Clients. We have a team of highly experienced and skilled professionals, state of arts equipment and world class certification to provide diving services in various aspects; underwater engineering, project management, marine support, consultancies etc. all under one roof. Scuba Sharks Uganda offers professional, prompt, efficient and competitive services. Our main strengths are loyalty, flexibility and ability to deliver the projects safely, on time and to the best possible quality.

WHO WE ARE

Professional divers at depth.

Scuba Sharks Uganda is an aquatic and diving services company focused on helping people learn, work and respond confidently in and around the water.

Our services span training and certification, underwater inspection, installation, cleaning and maintenance, shallow-water search and recovery, and emergency response training.

We believe good underwater work begins before the diver enters the water: understand the objective, assess the conditions, plan the operation, communicate clearly and work within safe limits.

OUR APPROACH

What we stand for.

Safety

We put preparation, communication, equipment checks and appropriate operating limits at the centre of our work.

Competence

We value proper skills development, continuous learning and matching the right capability to each task.

Reliability

Clients need clear communication, realistic expectations and dependable follow-through. That's how we aim to work.

Respect for Water

We operate with respect for the aquatic environment and the people who depend on it.

Buikwe, Uganda.
